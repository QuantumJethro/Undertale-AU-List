  • Beforehand, you should also know about the AU logic.
  • What is AU logic? This is when you take two AUs and combine them. This can also be more than one.
  • Let's take an populair example: Fellswap and Swapfell.
  • Now, these two are very different from each other. How are they different?
  • Fellswap is Underfell getting swapped.
  • Swapfell is Underswap getting feliffied.
  • Another example is Dustfell and Felldust.
  • Felldust is Underfell going through the events of Dusttale.
  • Dustfell is Dusttale getting fellified.
  • And, this is applied to everything.
  • The begin name of an AU stack is the base, the second or whatever is what happens after it.
